- **Q9 RESOLVED (user):** `serde_json` approved for machine-readable output.

## M2 additions (electron transport, option2)
- **Q4 (update)** The cumulated "fast" tables are what M2 uses and what option2 uses (`SetDNAFast(true)`). The slow rejection-sampling path (`G4DNABornIonisationModel1::RandomizeEjectedElectronEnergy`) and the statistical equivalence check against it were **not** implemented in M2 (out of the agreed scope); the `sigmadiff_ionisation_e_born.dat` table is loaded by `dna-data` but unused. Open until someone needs option2 with `DNAFast(false)`.
- **Q12 (new, needs a decision for M3)** Atomic de-excitation after oxygen K-shell ionisation is active in Geant4's option2 (docs/02 B.7.4 item 5) and **not simulated** in M2: the whole 539 eV is booked as local deposit and every occurrence is counted (`quirks.k_shell_deexcitation_skipped`). Effect size, from the partial cross sections only (hand computation at 4321 eV, `scripts/hand_cross_sections.py`): the K shell is 0.45 % of the ionisations, but each books 539 eV, so it supplies 2.4 eV of the 17.0 eV mean binding energy booked per ionisation (14 %); Geant4 books only what the Auger electron / fluorescence photon does not carry away (their energies come from the `fluor`/`auger` data, not examined). The *total* energy is unaffected (the Auger energy would be deposited later by the secondary), the deposit/secondary split of the primary track is. Not measured: a number needs running both variants. Options: (a) implement the K-shell Auger/fluorescence from the `G4EMLOW` `fluor`/`auger` data (adds data files and a model), (b) a documented simplified model (single Auger electron of fixed energy), (c) keep the deviation and compare with Geant4 run with fluo/auger off (what the M2 validation does). Default until decided: (c).
- **Q13 (new, needs a decision)** Geant4's Champion elastic model rotates with a non-unit axis (docs/02 B.7.4 item 3), so the deflection angle is smaller than the tabulated one for any direction off the coordinate axes (`a^2` between 2/3 and 1) and the Geant4 mean free path to a given depth, range and angular spread depend on how the beam is oriented. Rust reproduces it by default (`ElasticRotation::Geant4`) so that comparisons with Geant4 are meaningful, and offers `Exact`. Per AGENTS.md ("agreement with Geant4 alone does not guarantee physical correctness") the physically sound choice is `Exact`; whether to make it the default for science runs, and to report the size of the effect on range and G(t) by running both, is open. Both variants are covered by tests (`final_state.rs`).
- **Q14 (new)** M2 stops an electron whose energy is below the lowest inelastic window (9 eV) although elastic scattering remains possible between 7.4 and 9 eV: without Sanche vibrational excitation and Melton attachment (M3) it would scatter elastically forever. The remainder is booked as `remaining_untracked` and the termination reason (`NoInelasticChannel` / `BelowLowestWindow` / `TrackingCut` / `InteractionLimit`) is reported. M3 replaces both by the Geant4 processes (solvation at 7.4 eV). Results that depend on what happens below ~9 eV (full range, W-value, thermalisation positions) are not meaningful in M2.
- **Q15 (new)** The Born tables return `W - B < 0` or `k < B` only through the synthetic cases of the tests; with the committed data neither the `ionisation_noop_below_binding`, `transfer_clamped_to_zero` nor `excitation_noop` counters should ever be non-zero except `excitation_noop` for primaries of a few eV above 9 eV (a level with `E_exc >= E`). If a production run reports non-zero values, inspect the table interval; they are counted in the JSON `summary.quirks`.
- **Risk R1 (update)** The `SampleSecondaries` semantics of the three electron models are now ported line by line and tested against the raw tables (V4), against analytic formulas and against the reproduced quirks; what remains unverified is agreement with an actual Geant4 run (L3), pending the reference CSVs.
- **Risk R11 (new, medium)** The reference generator `validation/geant4/g4rs_ref` was never compiled. If it silently deviates (e.g. fluo/auger still on, secondaries not killed) the comparison can fail or pass for the wrong reason; the CSV header lists the settings and the README lists what to check.

## M3 additions (secondaries, Sanche, Melton, solvation)
- **Q14 CLOSED by M3.** The elastic-only termination guard and the `NoInelasticChannel`/`BelowLowestWindow` reasons are gone; electrons between 7.4 and 9 eV are removed by Sanche (energy walk-down) and Melton (absorption) and everything at or below 7.4 eV is solvated. Test: `transport.rs::electrons_between_7_4_and_9_ev_are_removed_by_the_low_energy_processes`.
- **Q12 (update).** K-shell ionisations are 0.25 % of all ionisations of a 1 MeV cascade (2357 of ~9.5e5 over 20 histories, docs/08), each booking 539 eV locally. Still open whether to simulate Auger/fluorescence; the Geant4 M3 reference is generated with it off so the comparison is like with like. The K shell supplies 0.25 % x 539 eV = 1.3 eV of the mean binding energy booked per ionisation (about 14 eV, i.e. about 9 %): energy that would reappear as Auger-electron kinetic energy and ionise further.
- **Q13 (update, numbers).** For the same streams the two rotations give bitwise identical energies, path lengths, counts, spectra and W; only positions differ. Paired difference of the primary's range `Exact - Geant4`: -0.48 % at 1 keV (4 sigma), -1.16 % at 10 keV (4.6 sigma), not resolved at 100 keV and 1 MeV with 400 and 40 histories. Radial profile and G(t) effects still to be quantified with the chemistry (M7/M8). Decision about the default for science runs still open; the default remains `Geant4`.
- **Q16 (new, needs a decision).** Lower limit of primaries. Rust accepts primaries from 2 eV (the lowest cross-section window); below the lowest window nothing is validated for a *primary*, although Geant4 solvates any electron below 7.4 eV. Secondaries are not restricted. Recommended: keep 2 eV.
- **Q17 (new, M6).** Solvation penetration law. Only `Meesungnoen2002` (Geant4's default) is implemented; `Terrisol1990`, `Ritchie1994`, `Kreipl2009`, `Meesungnoen2002_amorphous` and the chemistry-on details (25 meV, navigator) belong to M6. The `r_mean` polynomial is a fit to data above 0.2 eV; its use up to 7.4 eV and the 1e-3 nm branch below 0.1 eV (0.8 % of the solvations) are Geant4 behaviour, not validated physics. Mean displacement of the solvated electron found: 11.3-11.5 nm.
- **Q18 (new, informational).** The commented-out Sanche kill-and-deposit branch (docs/02 B.7.5) suggests an earlier version removed electrons below the lower limit; in 11.4.1 nothing happens when `E - E_vib <= 0`, which cannot occur in the window. No action unless the window is widened.
- **Q19 (new).** `tracking_cut` applies to the primary only; secondaries of a cut primary are still followed when `transport_secondaries` is on. The Geant4 `track` reference kills secondaries instead (hence `transport_secondaries = false` in the M2 gate). A "track-segment" mode in the sense of `chem6`'s `PrimaryKiller` (docs/01 A.4 item 4) is not implemented; it belongs to M8.
- **R1 (update).** The final-state semantics of Sanche, Melton and the thermalisation are now ported line by line and tested against the raw tables, hand computations and a scripted RNG (docs/02 B.7.5); their agreement with an actual Geant4 run is pending (L3).
- **R11 (closed).** The M2 generator was compiled and run by the project lead; the CSV headers record the settings (fluo/Auger off, option2). The new `slow` and `xslow` modes are **untested C++** (syntax-checked against stub headers only).
- **R12 (new, medium) Cost of full cascades.** A 1 MeV electron is 2.6e6 interactions and 4.7e4 secondaries (about 1.5 s on one core); 10^4 such histories are about 4 core-hours. The physicochemical record has about 1e5 events (~1e6 B/history as CSV text) per 1 MeV history, so event storage must stay streamed (it is, `PhysChemSink`), and M6 should consume events per history without keeping them. Mitigation: cheap low-energy steps dominate (1.8e6 interactions/s), so the profile is flat and a profile-guided optimisation belongs to M9.
- **R13 (new, medium) W below expectation.** The a-priori estimate of W (central 25 eV) was about 20 % above the result (19.5-21.0 eV). Not a failure of a pre-registered test, but an unexplained gap between my reasoning and the code: either the reasoning was too crude (likely: the gas-phase W/I does not transfer) or a model detail is off. The Geant4 `slow` reference (number of ionisations per event) decides.

## M6 part A additions (physicochemical stage)
- **Q20 (new, needs a decision for session B).** The five excitation relaxation channels (A^1B_1 0.35, B^1A_1 0.175, the three Rydberg layers 0.5) book the excitation energy a second time as a *chemistry-stage* local deposit (`DecayIt`). **Correction from the 2026-10-16 source re-audit:** `H2Ovib` relaxation (0.35) has no `SetEnergy` and deposits zero; Rust already implements this correctly (docs/02 B.7.8). `dna-chemistry` reports decay energy separately (`PhysChemStats::decay_energy_ev`) and does **not** add it to `E_dep` of docs/01 A.4: the IT stepping code never calls the sensitive detector, so `chem6` does not score it (docs/02 B.7.6 item 2; grep evidence, not exhaustive). To be confirmed against the `chem6` energy in session B (compare `E_dep` with and without it).
- **Q21 (new, medium) Order of the water molecules in the electron-hole recombination.** Which hole takes which `e_aq` depends on the order in which Geant4 steps the molecules at 1 ps (its track list), which the source read does not fix. `dna-chemistry` creates all solvated electrons first, then processes the water molecules in the event order of the cascade (auto-ionisation electrons become available to later holes). Session B must measure the sensitivity (shuffle the event order and compare the recombination fraction and the species counts) and compare the initial yields with `chem6`.
- **Q22 (new, informational) Birth time of the initial species is 1 ps, not 0.** Geant4 places the water molecules and the solvated electrons at `picosecond` and the decay products at the global time of the mother (docs/02 B.7.6 item 1). `SpeciesInstance::time` is 1 ps for every species; the G(t) output times of the chemical stage are global times, so the first output time of `chem6` is 1 ps.
- **Q23 (new, low) Two temperatures.** The Onsager radius uses the material temperature 293.15 K (`NTP_Temperature`, eps = 80.10, r = 0.7116 nm); the chemistry uses 298 K. Reproduced as is. At 298 K the radius would be 0.7151 nm (+0.5 %, hand evaluation), far below the statistical resolution of the recombination fraction expected in session B.
- **R14 (new, medium) Recombination fraction rests on assumptions.** At 10 keV, 22 % of the ionised molecules recombine (2132 of 9830 over 20 histories; 82 % have an `e_aq` within 7.1 nm), which lowers G(e_aq) and G(H3O+) and raises G(H2), G(H) and G(O). This is the strongest lever of the stage and depends on Q21 and on the projection of `G4DNAElectronHoleRecombination` onto a per-history ordering. Mitigation: compare initial species counts with Geant4 `chem6` at 1 ps (session B), run with recombination off for the ablation.
- **R15 (new, low) Reproduction of Geant4 quirks.** `OH-` gets no displacement in the dissociative attachment (distinct molecule definition); the attachment products `H2 + OH- + OH` balance only with an extra water; the relax channels deposit energy again. All reproduced and documented (docs/02 B.7.6), none corrected.
- **Q24 CLOSED (lifecycle only).** The `g4rs_ref physchem` lifecycle crash is fixed; the M6 comparison remains open. The first positive-step pre-callback follows zero-time chemical reactions as well as dissociation (Q25), so the reference's historical "before diffusion/reactions" label is inaccurate. See `validation/geant4/BUILD_NOTES.md` section 5, issues G-K, for lifecycle fixes, and the source audit and complete comparison in docs/08 for snapshot semantics.
- **R16 (new, medium) Initial spatial-correlation definition.** M6 reports same-decay OH--e_aq and OH--H3O+ distances. This is reproducible and O(N), but it is not a track-wide pair-correlation function. If M7 validation requires an RDF or nearest-neighbour statistic, pre-register bins/cutoffs and add it as a distinct observable rather than silently changing this one.
- **Q25 (evidenced 2026-10-16, comparison boundary unresolved).** The existing Geant4 reference at 1 ps includes option3 zero-time contact reactions; Rust M6 ends after option1 dissociation and electron-hole recombination. `source/processes/electromagnetic/dna/management/src/G4Scheduler.cc:550-552,613-626,663-701` selects a step before its pre-callback and executes reactions on preceding zero steps. `source/processes/electromagnetic/dna/management/src/G4ITModelProcessor.cc:118-131,186-197,336-354` selects IRT collisions and applies their changes. `source/processes/electromagnetic/dna/models/src/G4DNAIRT.cc:298-333,527-548` permits zero contact times and reacts at the current global time. The dissociation products in `source/physics_lists/constructors/electromagnetic/src/G4ChemDissociationChannels_option1.cc:194-465` exclude H2O2, HO2°, Om (O^-), and O2; their chemical paths are in `G4EmDNAChemistry_option3.cc:170-187,255-287` in the same directory. They appear at every reference energy; rare HO2m, O2m and O3 also appear. Source version is Geant4 11.4.1; exact call order and all 60 comparison rows are in docs/08. Establish a matching comparison boundary before interpreting the count failure as a dissociation/displacement defect.
- **R17 (new, high) Mixing physicochemical and chemical snapshots.** Identical global time does not imply identical stage: zero-time reactions change populations and reaction-site positions before any positive-time step. The current CSV/provenance and generator comment are preserved historical evidence, despite their inaccurate "before reactions" wording. The focused `physchem_reference.rs` diagnostic distinguishes the seven dissociation products from the observed reaction-only species. M6 remains open; count z <= 4.5, the 15% count limit for non-small means and the 20% RMS limit are unchanged. Any corrected or additional reference must use new files and a distinct generator mode unless a separately documented bug fix is authorized; only the user runs Geant4.
- **Q21/R14 measured, still open (2026-10-16).** The source re-audit found no channel or timing defect. Five water-order ablations on identical physical histories give recombination ranges 24.1294-25.4300%, 20.6775-21.7900%, 19.2507-20.2541%, 18.7887-19.8429% at 1/10/100/900 keV, respectively (seed 20261017; 20000/2000/200/20 histories). At 10 keV, recombination off raises mean e_aq from 400.0000 to 502.3895 and lowers H from 85.8750 to 17.1175. Full means, paired uncertainties and reproducible command are in docs/04 D.2.1. Event-order processing remains a documented assumption: C++ `FindReactant` caches a candidate list before `MakeReaction` tests surviving candidates, whereas Rust searches immediately before each decay (source paths in docs/02 B.7.8). Reverse events alone does not reproduce that scheduler. The comparison report remains unchanged; these measurements cannot resolve Q25/R17 or close M6 without a matching stage boundary.

## Decisions confirmed 2026-10-16 (gps roadmap session)
- **Q24 CLOSED.** The `g4rs_ref physchem` crash is fixed (IRT time-step model set explicitly, snapshot at the first scheduler step with `dt > 0`, secondaries transported, atomic de-excitation off); see `validation/geant4/BUILD_NOTES.md` section 5 (issues G-K). Reference data: `validation/reference/physchem.csv`, `provenance_m6.toml`. The **M6 gate is still open**: `1000 eV H2O2: z = 111.6` (docs/08, seed `m6-comparison-report`).
- **Snapshot interpretation corrected by the M6 audit.** The callback above is after zero-time chemistry, not just after dissociation (Q25/R17). This does not reopen the lifecycle crash or alter the committed reference, original gate tolerances, or M6 closure requirement.
- **M7 order.** M7 implements option3 reactions first (`docs/gps-backlog.md`).
- **R16 stays.** The M6 same-decay pair statistic stays distinct from any future RDF/nearest-neighbour observable; such an observable is added separately with pre-registered bins and cutoffs.
- **Kept unless the M6 reference contradicts them:** Q12 (atomic de-excitation skipped), Q13 (Geant4 elastic rotation default), Q16 (2 eV floor), Q17 (Meesungnoen2002 only).
- **M6 before M7.** M6 closes before M7 consumes its species and positions (initial conditions); only `m7-option3-reaction-table` is independent.
- **M8 scope.** The proton 10 MeV acceptance is deferred until M5; M8 seeds cover e- 100 keV and 1 MeV.
- **Geant4 runs.** Agent sessions write and compile-check C++ but never run it; runs are requested in `validation/geant4/RUN_REQUESTS.md`.
- No ADR written: none of these is hard to reverse and surprising at once.
